### 1. Client Acquisition & Outreach

**Cold Email Personalization Engine**
```
Act as an expert B2B sales copywriter specializing in [your service].
Write a short, personalized cold email to [prospect company/role].
Context about prospect: [insert research: recent post, funding, pain point].
My offer: [one-sentence value prop].
Goal: Book a 15-min discovery call.
Tone: Professional, concise, no hype.
Structure: Hook with observation, 1-sentence value, soft CTA.
Keep under 120 words.
```

**LinkedIn Outreach Sequence (3 messages)**
```
Create a 3-message LinkedIn outreach sequence for a [target role, e.g. agency owner] who struggles with [specific pain].
Message 1: Soft connection request with observation.
Message 2 (after accept): Value-first insight + soft offer.
Message 3: Direct but low-pressure CTA for a call.
Make each under 100 words. Avoid salesy language.
```

**Niche Opportunity Scanner**
```
Analyze the current market for AI-assisted [service type, e.g. content writing, automation setup] targeted at [niche, e.g. e-commerce brands under $5M].
List 5 underserved pain points, estimated willingness to pay, and a one-sentence productized offer for each.
Base on realistic 2026 demand.
```
